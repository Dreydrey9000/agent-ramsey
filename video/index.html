Meet Agent Ramsey, your Mac's resource agent. He finds what is busy, so your next job has room to run.
Ramsey groups apps with their helpers, measures CPU over time, and checks memory pressure first.
He protects active and unknown work, including renders, uploads, recordings, and calls. He never closes apps automatically.
Before a heavy job, give him a memory and CPU budget. He checks whether to go, wait, or investigate.
He also teams up with Quicksilver. Ramsey checks resources. Quicksilver finds one app bottleneck, makes one safe fix, and measures again.
Onboard once. Ramsey checks every five minutes after login, stays quiet when things are healthy, and runs no resident AI model.
